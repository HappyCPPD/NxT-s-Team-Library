# Flack Safety

In your home country, the Peaceful Paddle of Platypuses (PPP), the government has been placing AI (sorry -- SI) powered security cameras in public spaces, for the "public safety".

During a recent riot, you were able to get your hands on one of the cameras, and found the IP address and `/etc/shadow` entry for an employee's workstation.

![Torn Down Camera](https://api.platypwn.ctf.platypwnies.de/v1/assets/675b4966-bb63-4dd3-9d3f-a589b29f9198?expiry=1794593393&token=Lot0WwUa4uvXYIu9YDkmVQBaAXMZFn5onVWThdl5R7NLlawFAkigrIZzyt59Eg1dqJstIJ1HBXpt1zvUNrj2dQ%3D%3D)

Progress (and your flags) show up on the internal backup dashboard, which you can reach from the workstation. It comes up a few minutes after your instance starts, once every backup exists.
