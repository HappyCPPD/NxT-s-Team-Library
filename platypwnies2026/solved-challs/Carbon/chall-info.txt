# Carbon

Platypus Freight lets its customers check their transport emissions in
**Carbon**, a shiny new customer portal. The ops team says the box behind it
was "cleaned up for SOC2" in September, and that no passwords are lying around
anymore.

Get a foothold through the portal, then work your way to root.

**Flags:** the user flag is in `/home/operator/user.txt`, the root flag in
`/root/root.txt`.

Reverse shells to your own machine are allowed for this challenge.


Task
Requires: LIVE INSTANCE -- start it on the platform (Instanz) before solving.
