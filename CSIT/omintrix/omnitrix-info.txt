Omnitrix
TISC LEVEL 7
description

Something has gone wrong inside the Singularity.

While tracing one of its older compromises, we found references to a Plumber facility in Bellwood. The facility disappeared from every modern database years ago, but its server is still responding.

The files mention an alien device, something called the Omnitrix, and a man named Vilgax who spent years trying to take it.

The strange part is that these records predate The Singularity by a decade.

And yet, the machine seems to know about them.

Whatever happened here, The Singularity has inherited something that was never supposed to be part of it.

Find out what.

Note: this is an instanced challenge, refer to the instructions in omnitrix-connector.zip to login. Use the same credentials as you would for this platform.
