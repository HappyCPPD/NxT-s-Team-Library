Description:
A satellite ground-support station runs its propellant fueling and cooling on
an aging GSC-7 field controller. Before the next payload test can be
authorized, the controller has to be validated — and right now it won't pass:
a safety interlock on the fueling sequence looks wrong, and the one engineer
who touched it last is unreachable.

You're the contracted security engineer. You have network access to the live
controller and a copy of its maintenance toolkit, including the controller
firmware image. Work out how the controller authenticates an engineer, find
and correct the altered safety logic, make the fix actually stick, and bring
the fueling/cooling test to a clean, safe completion. The controller issues a
validation token only on a genuinely successful run.

This is a facility-validation exercise: defensive work on launch-support
infrastructure. No targeting or weapons systems are involved.

The toolkit, firmware image, protocol reference, and control narrative are in
the download. The controller exposes a control service and a read-only
telemetry feed; see the reference document for ports and commands.
